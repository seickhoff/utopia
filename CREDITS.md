# Credits

- **Utopia** (1981) was designed and programmed by Don Daglow for Mattel Electronics' Intellivision. This remake is a fan tribute; the name, rules and original graphics belong to their rights holders.
- The game's rules, bitmaps and island map were decoded from the annotated disassembly by Joe Zbiciak (intvnut), published in the AtariAge thread "Utopia Revealed!" (2012), with his 2020 corrections.
- The Intellivision-style pixel font used for the classic view is original to this project, not the console's GROM.
- The sound effects are the cartridge's own. Its sound data was played through the console's EXEC sound routines on an emulated CP-1610 and AY-3-8914, following Joe Zbiciak's jzIntv emulator, and recorded once to the MP3s in `client/public/sounds/`.
